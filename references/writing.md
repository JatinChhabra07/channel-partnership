# Writing the templates and drafts

Load order before writing: `brain.py context` → **operator rules** → **campaign rules** → candidate lessons →
facts. Rules override your instincts. If a rule conflicts with a fact (e.g. "always mention the commission" but no
approved commission fact exists), the fact wins, and you tell the user.

## What a partner email has to do in ~100 words

1. **Why them, specifically** (one true line from their own site/listing): "You install access control for offices in Pune".
2. **What's in it for them**, in the partner's economics, not our features. For a referral/reseller partner
   that means extra revenue from clients they already have, a reason to go back to those clients, or a gap they currently
   can't fill. Use only the incentives the program facts allow.
3. **Why our product**: one proof point the partner's client would care about (a named customer or a number from the brain).
4. **The smallest possible ask**: a reply, a 15-minute call, "should I send the 1-page overview?"
5. Opt-out line + signature.

## The 4-5 templates must differ in angle, e.g.

- **T1 Client-gap**: "Your clients ask for X alongside CCTV, and you can't offer it."
- **T2 Proof-led**: lead with a named customer in the partner's own market.
- **T3 Revenue**: the partner economics, only as far as program facts allow.
- **T4 Trigger**: a timely reason (regulation, a new office wave in their city, a last30days finding), only if real.
- **T5 Ultra-short**: 3 sentences, a question as the subject.

Don't produce five rewordings of one email. If two of your templates would get the same reply rate for the same
reason, merge them and find a new angle.

## Style defaults (until the brain's rules say otherwise)

- Plain text, no bullet lists, no bold, no links in email 1 (or one link at most).
- Subject: 2-6 words, lowercase-ish, specific ("visitor logs for your CCTV clients"), no clickbait, no "Quick question".
- No AI tells: "I hope this finds you well", "I came across", "leverage", "seamless", "game-changer",
  "not just X, it's Y", em-dash chains, triads of adjectives.
- Indian English context where the recipient is Indian (₹, lakh, local city names) only when it's true and natural.

## Placeholders

`{{company}}`, `{{first_name}}` (only if a real name was published; otherwise use a neutral greeting, never "Hi there friend"),
`{{city}}`, `{{service}}` (their service, from evidence), `{{evidence_line}}` (the one personalised sentence),
`{{proof}}` (segment-matched proof from the brain), `{{sender_name}}`, `{{signature}}`.

## Assignment syntax the user may use

"T2 for everyone" · "first 20 T1, rest T3" · "T1 for CCTV, T4 for facility management" · "A/B T1 vs T5 on CCTV".
Convert it to an explicit list (prospect → template) and confirm in one line. For A/B, alternate within each segment
so the arms are comparable.

## Follow-ups

One follow-up, 4-6 working days later, on the same thread, ≤50 words, adds one new piece of value (not "just bumping").
Never follow up on replied, bounced or opted-out threads. The ledger statuses tell you.
